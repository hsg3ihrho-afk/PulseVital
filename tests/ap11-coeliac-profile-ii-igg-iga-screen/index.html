Coeliac Profile II – IgG + IgA Screen

Also known as coeliac blood test, celiac disease test or coeliac antibody test.

Common questions

How long do the results take?

Results for this test come back in 5 days. Allergy tests take five days; other tests take three.

Is the home visit included in the price?

Yes. £134.30 includes the phlebotomist visiting your address. There is no extra call-out fee.

Which areas do you cover?

Oxfordshire, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Wiltshire, Northamptonshire, Gloucestershire and Warwickshire. See every town we cover. The booking form checks your postcode.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before your appointment window starts. After that the fee is non-refundable, because your phlebotomist's time is reserved for you. Read our cancellation policy.

How should I prepare?

Drink plenty of water before your visit, as it makes the sample easier to take. If you have a dog, please keep it in another room.
