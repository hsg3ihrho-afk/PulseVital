Cancellation policy

We keep this simple, because every booking reserves a phlebotomist's time just for you.

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

You can cancel any booking free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment window starts. Cancel in your account, or email hello@pulsevital.co.uk.

Less than 24 hours before

If you cancel less than 24 hours before your appointment window starts, the fee is non-refundable. By then we have booked a phlebotomist for you and turned away other appointments in that slot.

When your window starts

Morning appointments start at 8:00 AM and afternoon appointments start at 12:00 PM, UK time. So for an afternoon visit on a Friday, you can cancel free of charge until 12:00 PM on Thursday.

Questions about a booking? Email hello@pulsevital.co.uk.
